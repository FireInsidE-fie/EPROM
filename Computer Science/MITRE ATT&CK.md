The MITRE ATT&CK (*Adversarial Tactics, Techniques, and Common Knowledge*) framework is a **comprehensive global knowledge repository** that meticulously documents the **tactics and techniques employed by threat actors at every stage of a cyber-attack**.
It functions as a strategic playbook, providing detailed insights into attackers' methods, from **gaining initial access** to **maintaining a presence**, **escalating privileges**, **evading defenses**, **moving laterally within a network**, and much more.
It is developed and maintained by the [[MITRE Corporation]].
# Use Cases
- [[Threat Modeling]]
- Identifying potential attack paths based on an organization's infrastructure.
  Since the ATT&CK framework maps techniques to technologies, you can focus on the ones an organization uses to map paths a malicious actor might try to take in your infrastructure.
- Developing threat scenarios based on real threat groups.
- Prioritizing vulnerability remediation.
  Taking example on real-world attacks that have happened in the past helps prepare for if a similar attack does occur for your organization.
- ...many more. The ATT&CK framework is an amazing treasure trove of information. Use it wisely.
# Structure
The ATT&CK framework is organized into a **matrix that covers various tactics (high-level objectives), as well as techniques (methods used to achieve those objectives)**.
The framework **includes descriptions, examples, and mitigations** for each mentioned technique.
It provides a **detailed overview** of the threat actors' methods and tools.
