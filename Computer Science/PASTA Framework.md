PASTA (*Process for Attack Simulation and Threat Analysis*), is a **structured, risk-centric [[Threat Modeling]] framework designed to help organizations identify and evaluate security threats and vulnerabilities in their systems, apps, or infrastructure**.
It provides a **seven step process to help security teams understand potential attack scenarios better, as well as assess and prioritize threats**.
# Methodology
1. **Define the Objectives**: Establish the scope of the threat modeling exercise.
   What systems are to be audited? What are the objectives in compliance and security?
2. **Define the Technical Scope**: Create an inventory of assets to better understand the infrastructure.
3. **Decompose the Application**: Break down the system into components.
   Identify entry points, trust boundaries, potential attack surfaces.
   Map out the data flows and understand user roles and privileges.
4. **Analyze the Threats**: Identify the threats and classify them.
5. **Vulnerability and Weakness Analysis**
6. **Analyze the Attacks**: Simulate attack scenarios and evaluate their likelihood and impact.
   Rank the threats by risk and prioritize.
7. **Risk and Impact Analysis**: Develop security controls and countermeasures.
   These should be aligned with the organization's risk tolerance and security objectives.
# Benefits
- Adaptable to unique objectives and most organizations.
- Fosters collaboration between stakeholders (developers, architects, security professionals)
- Helps organizations meet compliance of regulations
- Comprehensive and systematic process
