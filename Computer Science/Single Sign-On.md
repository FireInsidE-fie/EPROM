Single Sign-On or SSO makes it so that **instead of having to login to multiple platforms through different passwords or methods, you sign in once to a service that will then authenticate you to all other systems necessary**.
# Advantages
SSO has multiple advantages:
- **Having only one password to remember**: this allows you to make it extra strong, and to change it more often
- **Easier MFA**
- **Simpler Support**: password resets suddenly become a lot simpler
- **Efficiency**: you gain time by not having to login to every service under the sun separately
