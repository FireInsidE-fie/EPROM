An ASIC is **a chip designed and hardwired to do one specific job**.
It is often used in [[Switch]] devices to forward frames very, very fast, or in graphics cards.
The main benefit is that general-purpose [[Processor]]s would be too slow to work on a specific job, so you **create a specialized chip that does only that one job**.
