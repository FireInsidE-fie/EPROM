SOC 2 is a **framework for checking an organization's compliance and auditing processes**.
It focuses on **assessing a company's data security based on the CIA triad**.
It certifies that **a company has sufficient controls in place to safeguard its systems, data, and sensitive information**.
# Key Points
- Evaluates the usefulness of an organization's controls in regards to confidentiality, availability, integrity, and privacy
- Independent auditors conduct SOC 2 audits
- SOC 2 reports are a treasure trove of information for customers, stakeholders, and regulators.
  They describe the organization's security and privacy practices.
  As such, they can be used to demonstrate that an organization has good controls to protect its users' personal information.
  Reports often include recommendations on how to do better.
  They can be shared with customers and stakeholders to ensure the company takes appropriate action.
- Audits can cover physical security, network security, data encryption, backup, and recovery, as well as employee training and awareness
