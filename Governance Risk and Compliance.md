The GRC framework is **an approach to security in an organization that aligns with the organization's goals and objectives, and helps to ensure that the organization operates within regulations and industry standards**.
# Components
The GRC framework has **three components**.
## Governance
**Guiding an organization** by setting its direction through a specific information security strategy.
Strategies usually include **policies, standards, baselines, frameworks, etc...**
They also set **appropriate monitoring to measure its performance and look at the big picture**.
## Risk Management
This is all about **identifying, assessing, and prioritizing risks to the organization**.
Once risks are classified, it also involves **implementing controls and mitigation strategies to manage those risks effectively**.
Often, this materializes as **monitoring and reporting** on risks, and continuously evaluating and refining the risk management program, to make sure it's still effective over a longer period.
## Compliance
Compliance means **ensuring that the organization follows laws, regulations, and other industry obligations or standards**.
This includes developing and implementing compliance programs, conducting audits regularly, and reporting on issues to management.
# Developing a GRC Program
1. Define the scope and objectives
2. Conduct a risk assessment: Define what risks an organization has, to be able to mitigate them later.
3. Develop policies and procedures
4. Establish governance processes: Make sure the GRC program will be maintained and controlled.
5. Implement controls
6. Monitor and measure performance
7. Continuously improve
