$L^AT_EX$ is a **tool for typesetting professional-looking documents**.
It is great for documents that contain **complex mathematics or general technical content**.
# How It Works
The idea is simple: you edit your plaintext document, adding *LaTeX commands* to it, and a *TeX engine* will then compile your document into a professional-looking [[PDF]] file.
